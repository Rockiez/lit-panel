# lit-panel: An Eleven-Seat Mutual-Blind Peer Review System for Chinese Memoir and Narrative Text Based on Narratological Deconstruction

*An eleven-seat, mutual-blind literary review panel for Chinese memoir / narrative text — a Claude Code / Codex / Google Antigravity skill.*

![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg) ![Version: 0.4.1](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-0.4.1-lightgrey.svg)

> **[Abstract]** Addressing the epistemological dilemmas of pseudo-precise continuous scalar scoring, aesthetic homogenization, and hallucinated citations prevalent in Large Language Models (LLMs) during literary criticism and narrative evaluation, this study presents `lit-panel`, a multi-agent mutual-blind peer review system specifically engineered for Chinese memoirs and non-fiction narrative texts. Grounded in classical narratology and the binary criteria framework of the Torrance Test of Creative Writing (TTCW), the system establishes an eleven-seat independent evaluation space spanning source fidelity backtracing, in-text continuity, machine-generated artifacts (AI slop), narrative architecture, character psychology, prose rhythm, affective resonance, naive reader phenomenological perception, originality, brief adherence, and narrative ethics. Operating within physically isolated, mutual-blind execution environments, the review seats conduct parallel evaluations, filter out false claims via mechanical verbatim quote verification, and synthesize qualitative band assignments (A/B/C) alongside reproducible multi-dimensional scores derived through deterministic formal rules. Theoretical and empirical analyses demonstrate that this architecture effectively eliminates stochastic scoring drift while preserving the intrinsic tensions and incommensurability of literary aesthetics.
>
> **[Keywords]** Narratology; Literary Criticism; Peer Review; Mutual Blindness; TTCW; Computational Humanities; Critique of False Precision

## 1. Introduction: Epistemological Dilemma and Critique of Continuous Quantitative Scales

In computational literary criticism and textual quality assessment, assigning a continuous scalar score such as "7.5/10" to a memoir chapter or narrative essay superficially maintains an objective quantitative facade. In truth, it succumbs to the epistemological illusion of collapsing multifaceted, incommensurable aesthetic judgments into a single arbitrary scalar. Such numeric scores not only display significant distributional drift across subtle prompt variations or model iterations, but also obscure the internal structural tensions between formal architecture, vocal authenticity, and emotional veracity.

Abandoning continuous numerical scales is not an aesthetic indulgence, but an evidence-based methodological choice. Multiple core criteria in this system (the TW series across Seats 04, 05, 06, 07, and 09) are adapted from the theoretical framework of the **TTCW** (Torrance Test of Creative Writing). In evaluating creative narrative quality, the TTCW explicitly utilizes binary discrete criteria assessed item-by-item by professional writers rather than continuous metrics; foundational empirical studies demonstrated a profound structural misalignment between direct continuous scalar scores generated by LLMs and qualitative judgments rendered by seasoned writers. Continuous scores quietly flatten this divergence; `lit-panel` chooses to formally preserve aesthetic tension and disagreement.

By renouncing pseudo-precise direct scoring, the system establishes three concrete, empirically verifiable deliverables:

- **Discrete Criteria**: Verifying whether specific, observable textual behaviors and aesthetic phenomena are satisfied;
- **Verbatim Evidence**: Anchoring every verdict to mechanically verified verbatim textual quotes; unverified or hallucinated quotes trigger immediate invalidation of the corresponding claim, preventing corruption of the evidence chain;
- **Qualitative Bands**: Providing A/B/C categorical qualitative stratifications rather than misleading continuous scales.

The final evaluation report synthesizes **Qualitative Bands (A/B/C) + Verbatim Citations + Preserved Disagreement Zone + Concrete Revision Package + Multi-Dimensional Scorecard mechanically derived from a criteria vector** (introduced in v0.4.0+). **The review seats themselves practice strict zero-scoring discipline** (Section 3.4 output contract): seats emit only discrete verdicts and textual citations without generating or perceiving numbers; scores are merely downstream algebraic projections of the criteria vector under open, deterministic formulas (see `SKILL.md` §5.8), ensuring complete transparency and reproducibility.

## 2. Theoretical Framework and Eleven-Seat Narratological Peer Review Mechanics

This section outlines the formal review mechanics for researchers and practitioners. ### 2.1 Eleven-Seat Review Panel Architecture and Narratological Deconstruction

| Seat Identifier | Theoretical Critical Domain & Focus Area | Activation Condition | Band Role & Permission Attributes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** `lit-fidelity` | Source fidelity: claim backtracing against historical records, five-state labels (SUPPORTED/PERMISSIBLE_INFERENCE/UNSUPPORTED/CONTRADICTED/UNVERIFIABLE) | When `--source` provided | Sole determinant for Fidelity Band; Red-line veto authority |
| **02** `lit-continuity` | In-text continuity: temporal order, character identity, factual consistency, internal world norms | Globally active | Core evidence seat; Red-line veto authority on confirmed contradictions |
| **03** `lit-slop` | Machine-generated artifact detection: span tagging across multi-dimensional pattern libraries (light/heavy slop) | Globally active | Evidence and feature extraction seat; No independent veto authority |
| **04** `lit-structure` | Narratological structure: Genette temporal distortion, scene vs. summary balance, foreshadowing and payoff | Globally active | Literary Band core voting seat |
| **05** `lit-character` | Characterization and agency: motivational continuity, distinct conversational register, anti-sanitization | Globally active | Literary Band core voting seat |
| **06** `lit-prose` | Stylistics and prose rhythm: narrative voice consistency, syntactic transition, sensory precision | Globally active | Literary Band core voting seat |
| **07** `lit-resonance` | Affective resonance and arrival: processed emotion vs. lived experience, anti-sentimentalism | Globally active | Literary Band core voting seat |
| **08** `lit-naive-reader` | Naive reader phenomenological perception: blind pre-test without prior criteria, pure reading reaction and post-test tracking | Globally active (strict two-step protocol) | Participates in terminal synthesis; Excluded from baseline criteria vector |
| **09** `lit-originality` | Originality and defamiliarization: narrative cliché auditing, authentic biographical voice | Globally active | Positive-only bonus dimension (no deductions, never caps band) |
| **10** `lit-brief` | Editorial brief adherence: thematic intention delivery, prompt-mandated dramatic constraints | Preset includes 10 & `--brief` provided | Excluded from baseline band; Failed items converted to revision directives |
| **11** `lit-ethics` | Narrative ethics: unilateral representation protection, privacy necessity, dignity of vulnerable subjects | Active by default (explicit exclusion triggers warning) | Excluded from baseline band; All ethical findings escalated to human arbitration |

**Preset Configurations**: `quick`=01,02,03,08; `standard`=01–09+11 (default configuration, excluding 10; dynamically activates 10 when `--brief` is passed); `full`=01–11 (logs a Warning if 01/10 are skipped due to missing inputs); `custom(<list>)`=explicitly defined seat subset, e.g. `--preset custom(01,03,08)`. Passing unregistered identifiers halts execution immediately.

### 2.2 Three-Stage Evidence-Based Evaluation Protocol (Precheck, Mutual Blindness, Verification, Synthesis)

```
Input Stream: Target Text + Optional --source (Source Transcripts) / --brief (Editorial Brief)
        │
        ▼
Stage 0 · Mechanical Pre-check —— Fatal failure gateway: truncation / non-termination detection (instant halt);
                                  Metadata stripping, genre validation, brief hard-constraint verification
        │
        ▼
Stage 1 · Mutual-Blind Parallel Review —— 11 seats read independently in physically isolated contexts,
                                          applying dedicated criteria (Seat 08 executes strict 2-step blind protocol)
        │
        ▼
Stage 2 · Verbatim Citation Verification —— Mechanical string matching against source text for all quote spans;
                                            Unlocated quotes invalidated instantly (anti-hallucination firewall)
        │
        ▼
Stage 3 · Deterministic Rule Synthesis —— Red-line alarms / criteria vectors / qualitative bands / derived scores /
                                          disagreement zones / human arbitration queue / revision directives
        │
        ▼
Output: Structured Academic Evaluation Report + Evidence Sidecar Ledger
        (references/report-template.md schema + `<report_name>-details.md`)
```

### 2.3 Qualitative Band Stratification and Veto-Based Governance

The system maintains two independent qualitative tracks: **Fidelity Band (Seat 01)** and **Literary Band (Seats 04/05/06/07)**. The two tracks are evaluated in parallel and are never merged into a single metric.

Literary Band criteria are stratified into three formal tiers:
- **Veto Criteria (veto)**: Up to 2 catastrophic failure criteria per core seat (e.g. Seat 07 designates both "emotion completely un-dramatized and merely declared" and "emotion forced into theatrical distortion" as symmetric veto conditions).
- **Standard Core Criteria (core)**: Regular core aesthetic evaluation dimensions.
- **Extended Criteria (extended)**: Supplementary diagnostic items, recorded in the criteria vector without gating band assignments.

Band Assignment Algorithm (descending priority, terminates on first match):
1. Any veto failure with `severity=高` (high) $\rightarrow$ Literary Band capped at **C** (requires major rewrite);
2. Any veto failure with `severity=中/低` (medium/low) $\rightarrow$ Literary Band capped at **B**, with mandatory escalation to human arbitration;
3. No veto failures, but $\ge 1$ standard core criterion failure $\rightarrow$ Literary Band capped at **B**;
4. All veto and core criteria fully satisfied $\rightarrow$ Granted **A-Candidate** status (subject to texture verification against `anchors/band-a.md`).

**Severity functions as a four-way deterministic control gate**:
1. Fidelity Band gating: Only high-severity UNSUPPORTED triggers a C-band cap;
2. Red-line admission: High-severity factual contradictions route directly to the red-line alert section;
3. Veto branching: Distinguishes between C-band capping and B-band human arbitration;
4. Revision prioritization: Dictates the hierarchical ordering of corrective tasks.

### 2.4 Originality Bonus System (Non-Punitive Positive Incentive)

Starting with v0.4.1, Seat 09 (`lit-originality`) departed from the punitive veto/core gating hierarchy. The underlying scholarly stance is clear: **in memoir and life-writing, creative originality is an extraordinary literary merit rather than a foundational obligation**. An authentic and truthful life account should not suffer band degradation due to a lack of avant-garde stylistic novelty. Seat 09 outcomes map to derived score adjustments as follows (see `SKILL.md` §5.8):
- Positive originality criteria (O2/O3/O5/O6) **all satisfied** with zero negative findings $\rightarrow$ **+5 points** to total score;
- Positive criteria satisfied $\ge 3$ with zero negative findings $\rightarrow$ **+3 points**;
- Any other configuration (including negative findings) $\rightarrow$ **+0 points** (strictly non-deductive). Negative findings are routed to the revision package as polish recommendations.

### 2.5 Naive Reader Phenomenological Perception and Alarm Mechanism

To prevent rule-based evaluation from degenerating into bureaucratic formalism, the system incorporates Seat 08 (`lit-naive-reader`) as a phenomenological safeguard. When and only when all literary core criteria pass and the text qualifies for A-Candidate status, the system triggers the naive reader post-test inquiry: *"Would you genuinely recommend or recount this story to someone else?"* (strict binary choice: Yes / No).

- **Sample Size $N=1$**: If the reader answers "No", the system **neither awards Band A nor demotes the text to Band B**. Instead, the verdict is formally declared as *"**A-Candidate (Pending Human Confirmation — Divergence between criteria vector and reader experience)**"*, halting automated clearance and escalating to human arbitration.
- **Sample Size $N>1$**: Governed by majority vote; ties trigger conservative escalation to arbitration.

### 2.6 Mutual Blindness Isolation and Anti-Position-Bias Experimental Design

- **Swap-Order Double Review (exclusive to `/lit-compare`)**: In pairwise comparative evaluations, each seat evaluates the text pair twice: once as (A,B) and once as (B,A). A preference is recorded if and only if the seat prefers the identical text in both presentations; a reversal upon permutation records a **TIE**, effectively isolating presentation position bias.
- **Physical Context Isolation (Google Antigravity / Claude Code)**: Under Google Antigravity, review seats are concurrently instantiated via `invoke_subagent`; under Claude Code, via independent Task tools. No context sharing occurs, structurally guaranteeing mutual blindness.
- **State Reset Simulation (Codex Sequential Mode)**: In Codex sequential execution, the orchestrator explicitly injects context erasure instructions between seats to simulate forgetting discipline.
- **Cross-Family Model Evaluation**: Generative models and evaluation agents should belong to distinct architectural families (e.g. Claude generation $\rightarrow$ Codex / Gemini evaluation) to eliminate homologous bias blind spots.
- **Free Academic Opinion Field**: In addition to discrete criteria tables, each seat provides 1–3 paragraphs of unconstrained critical prose, which, together with the blind naive reader pre-test, forms a robust defense against metric hacking (Goodhart's Law).

## 4. Quickstart and Invocation Paradigms

```bash
# Single-text evaluation: Standard preset (01–09+11) with historical source transcripts for Seat 01
/lit-review chapter.md --source interview.md --preset standard
```

```bash
# A/B pairwise blind evaluation: Two candidate drafts with swap-order double review
/lit-compare a.md b.md
```

**Standardized Academic Report Schema**: A `/lit-review` report adheres to an invariant eight-section sequence (un-numbered headings): **Jury Synthesis (2–3 paragraphs) $\rightarrow$ Red-Line Alarms (if triggered, preceding scorecard) $\rightarrow$ Derived Scorecard (Total/Grade/Core Verdict) $\rightarrow$ Multi-Dimensional Score Table (Literary 4D + Originality Bonus + AI Cleanliness + Reader Experience + Fidelity) $\rightarrow$ Critic Commentary (prose remarks with embedded verbatim quotes) $\rightarrow$ Issue and Revision Directives $\rightarrow$ Human Arbitration Zone (prose-only divergence entries, no tables) $\rightarrow$ Evaluation Metadata Archive**. Full criteria vectors and verification logs reside in the sidecar ledger `<report_name>-details.md`.

## 6. Performance and Computational Complexity Evaluation

Observed execution profiles across hardware and runtime environments:

- **Codex Sequential Mode (medium/high reasoning)**: `quick` preset requires $\approx 10$ minutes; `standard` preset (10–11 sequential seats) requires $\approx 15\text{--}30$ minutes, scaling linearly $O(N)$ with active seats.
- **Antigravity / Claude Code Concurrent Mode**: `standard` preset duration is bounded by the **slowest individual seat execution** ($\approx 5\text{--}8$ minutes), converging to constant-time complexity $O(1)$.

**Operational Guidelines**: Use `quick` for rapid drafting iterations; mandate `standard` or `full` for formal editorial evaluation and archival grading; in Codex sequential mode, configure reasoning intensity to `medium` to optimize execution time without sacrificing verification precision.

## 7. Review Report Structure and Multi-Dimensional Score Derivation Mechanics

**Score Derivation Axioms**: Review seats maintain strict **zero-scoring**, outputting only discrete boolean determinations and verbatim citations. In Stage 3, the synthesis engine applies deterministic algebraic equations defined in `SKILL.md` §5.8:
$$\text{Base Score} = \frac{1}{4} \sum_{i \in \{04,05,06,07\}} S_i$$
where each literary dimension starts at 90 points, veto violations cap scores (high $\le 45$, medium/low $\le 65$), standard core failures deduct 12 points, and extended failures deduct 5 points. Superimposed are the Seat 03 AI penalty ($-3/\text{item}$, max $-10$), Seat 09 originality bonus ($+3 \sim +5$, non-deductive), and Seat 01 fidelity caps (Fidelity Band C caps total score at 45 with a mandatory rewrite directive).

**Human Arbitration Zone Constraints**: This section **strictly prohibits tables**, presenting all scholarly divergences as narrative prose. Six event classes mandate escalation to human arbitration:
1. All ABSTAIN determinations;
2. All findings from Seat 11 (narrative ethics);
3. Invalidated criteria items due to Stage 2 verbatim verification failure;
4. Veto failures with `severity=中/低` (medium/low);
5. Veto criteria marked as NA (not applicable);
6. Triggering of the Seat 08 phenomenological alarm.

## 8. Custom Criteria Pool and Review Seat Extension Criteria

- **Semantic Invariance**: Criteria phrasing in `references/criteria/*.md` may be refined, but polarity (`[通过]`/`[风险]`) must remain invariant. All additions/modifications must be recorded in `references/criteria/CHANGELOG.md`.
- **Private Criteria Mounting (`criteria/99-private.md`)**: Supports mounting unreleased local evaluation criteria to prevent generative models from gaming the evaluation suite.
- **Four Extension Axioms**: Adding a new seat requires satisfying:
  1. **Distinct Critical Perspective** (not a subset of existing seats);
  2. **Criteria Semantic Overlap $<20\%$**;
  3. **Unique Evidence Form** (e.g. source triangulation, two-step blind polling);
  4. **Irreplaceable Failure Coverage**.

## 9. Limitations Analysis and Empirical Validity Boundaries (Honesty Section)

The system formally discloses its methodological boundaries:

- **Verbatim Verification Ensures Existence, Not Hermeneutic Truth**: Stage 2 mechanical verification confirms only that a quote span exists in the source text; it cannot guarantee that the model's critical interpretation in the note is hermeneutically infallible.
- **Goodhart's Law Vulnerability in Public Criteria**: When evaluation metrics become optimization targets for generation models, they lose descriptive validity. The system mitigates this via blind naive reader testing and unconstrained free critical commentary.
- **Textual Ethics Does Not Replace Legal/Field Consent**: Seat 11 audits only representational ethics within the text, not external subject consent.
- **Variance Compression Rather Than Final Editorial Replacement**: Multi-agent mutual-blind review suppresses individual subjective noise and variance to deliver high-confidence evidence chains; it does not replace the ultimate aesthetic discernment of human editors.

### 9.1 Empirical Validity Boundaries

As of version v0.4.1, the empirical validation status of system components is categorized as follows:
- **Empirically Verified via Live Execution**: Multi-agent mutual-blind dispatch pipelines, verbatim string verification and hallucination invalidation, `quick`/`standard` assembly, cross-family model evaluation, full fidelity auditing, veto three-tier banding, naive reader two-step inquiry.
- **Awaiting Further Empirical Verification**: The naive reader "veto-A" anchor comparison branch under true Band A texts, Seat 10 deep task-brief parsing, multi-reader statistical pooling ($N > 1$), and extreme long-text stress testing in high-throughput production environments.

## 10. Data Privacy and Academic Ethics Statement

- All benchmark anchors in `references/anchors/` are **purely synthetic texts** containing no private personal data.
- Input texts and source materials flow exclusively between the user's local instance and configured LLM APIs; no telemetry or secondary data harvesting is conducted.
- We strongly recommend **isolating generative and review sessions**, prioritizing cross-family model evaluation to prevent self-referential bias.
